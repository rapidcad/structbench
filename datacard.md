# Dataset Card for StructData

## Dataset Details

### Dataset Description

StructData is a synthetic dataset of 147k structural load cases derived from 
the DeepCAD geometry corpus. Each instance pairs a CAD design domain with 
FEA-validated structural performance metrics, providing the first large-scale 
corpus specifying what a component must *do* rather than what it must *look like*. 
Every training instance specifies functional requirements — boundary conditions, 
applied loads, and design constraints — rather than geometric descriptors.

## Uses

### Direct Use

- Training generative CAD models on functional requirements rather than 
  geometric similarity
- Training surrogate models for topology optimisation — learning to predict 
  optimal material distributions from boundary conditions without running 
  iterative FEA solvers
- Training physics-informed neural networks that emulate FEA solutions from 
  problem specifications
- Evaluating structural performance prediction models that learn the mapping 
  from geometry to mechanical response

### Out-of-Scope Use

- Direct deployment of AI-generated designs in safety-critical structural 
  engineering applications without independent expert validation
- Training models for material selection (material is fixed to Al 6061 
  throughout; dataset does not support multi-material reasoning)
- Dynamic loading, fatigue, or contact mechanics tasks — FEA assumes linear 
  elasticity under static loading only

## Dataset Structure

Each instance contains:

| Field | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `domain` | dict | Axis-aligned bounding box defining design space (mm) |
| `boundary_conditions` | list | Fixed displacement regions with spatial extent |
| `loads` | list | Applied forces or pressures with direction and magnitude |
| `material` | dict | Material properties (Al 6061 throughout) |
| `natural_language_description` | string | Structured NL specification of the load case |
| `fea_result` | dict | FEA outcome: safety factor, von Mises stress, displacement |
| `cadquery_scaffold` | string | Executable CadQuery template with domain variables pre-filled |

**Splits:**

| Split | Size |
|---|---|
| Train | ~117,000 |
| Validation | ~1,500 |
| Test (StructBench) | 30 (hand-crafted, separate) |

## Dataset Creation

### Curation Rationale

No existing dataset pairs CAD geometry with structural FEA outcomes at scale. 
Existing datasets (DeepCAD, Fusion360, CADParser) contain only geometric 
information without functional annotations. StructData fills this gap to enable 
training of models that optimize for functional validity rather than geometric 
similarity.

### Source Data

#### Data Collection and Processing

Geometry derived from the DeepCAD dataset (MIT license). Each DeepCAD model 
defines a design domain. Boundary conditions and loads are assigned via 
geometry-aware heuristics: loads preferentially placed on axis-aligned planar 
faces, boundary conditions assigned to geometrically opposite or perpendicular 
faces, approximating the support-and-load topology of real engineering components 
such as cantilever brackets, beams, and pressure vessels. Force magnitudes and 
directions are sampled within ranges consistent with structural aluminium 
components. FEA performed with CalculiX (open-source, LGPL license).

**Load type distribution:** pressure (42%), distributed force (39%)  
**Domain volume range:** $10^{-2}$ to $10^3$ mm³ (five orders of magnitude)  
**Slenderness ratio range:** 1 to 250  

#### Who are the source data producers?

Geometry: DeepCAD dataset (Wu et al., ICCV 2021). Load cases: procedurally 
generated by the authors using geometry-aware heuristics. FEA annotations: 
computed automatically using CalculiX solver with no human annotation.

### Annotations

#### Annotation process

FEA annotations are computed automatically — no human annotators involved. 
Each load case is solved using CalculiX with material properties fixed to 
Al 6061. Coarse mesh (~5k elements) used for initial screening; fine mesh 
(~60k elements) for final annotation. Cases where FEA fails to converge are 
retained with failure mode recorded.

#### Who are the annotators?

Automated FEA pipeline (CalculiX solver). No human annotators.

#### Personal and Sensitive Information

None. All geometry is synthetic, derived from the DeepCAD corpus. No human 
subjects, personal data, or sensitive information of any kind.

## Bias, Risks, and Limitations

- **Geometry bias:** DeepCAD contains primarily consumer and hobbyist CAD 
  geometry (household objects, furniture). Industrial structural components 
  are underrepresented. Models trained on StructData may generalize poorly 
  to complex industrial geometries.
- **Physics scope:** FEA assumes linear elasticity under static loading only. 
  Dynamic loading, fatigue, plasticity, and contact mechanics are not captured.
- **Single material:** All cases use Al 6061. Models trained on StructData 
  cannot directly generalize to other materials without reward recentering.
- **Scale normalization:** Domain volumes are normalized to unit scale; 
  absolute dimensional reasoning may not transfer to real engineering scales.

### Recommendations

Designs generated using models trained on StructData must be validated by 
qualified structural engineers before use in any load-bearing application. 
The dataset is intended for research purposes only.

## Glossary

- **FEA:** Finite Element Analysis — numerical simulation method for 
  predicting structural behaviour under load
- **StructScore:** Composite metric $(1-\text{DSV}) \cdot \text{LPV} 
  \cdot \text{SFC} \cdot (1-\text{ME})$ measuring structural design quality
- **DSV:** Design Space Violation — fraction of generated material outside 
  the prescribed design domain
- **LPV:** Load Path Validity — binary indicator of material connectivity 
  between load and constraint regions
- **SFC:** Safety Factor Compliance — whether safety factor $\eta \geq 1$
- **ME:** Mass Efficiency — normalized fill ratio of generated geometry
